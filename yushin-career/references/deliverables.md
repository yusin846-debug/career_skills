# Application deliverables and execution

## 1. Build an evidence ledger first

Do not design a resume or portfolio until the factual architecture is stable. Record each candidate story with:

- situation and customer problem;
- action and commercial or operating decision;
- collaborators and personal ownership;
- result;
- period, denominator, and source;
- relevant application lane;
- confidence level and unresolved questions.

Reject a polished sentence when the underlying scope or number cannot be explained in an interview.

## 2. Resume architecture

Maintain one factual master record, then create a distinct resume for each lane. Do not position the target function as “founder.” Translate the same history differently without changing facts.

Default to a concise, one-page English resume for global roles unless the employer or local norm clearly calls for something else. Use bullets with this logic:

> problem or target → action and decision → measurable result → scope

Separate personal ownership from team outcome. Explain retention, renewal, or expansion logic instead of dropping unexplained percentages. Do not translate Korean bullets line by line when English business logic requires restructuring.

### Lane emphasis

- **Customer Success:** onboarding, usage or operating signals, renewal risk, commercial objection handling, expansion, cross-functional resolution.
- **Sales Ops / RevOps:** forecasting, pipeline accuracy, scenario analysis, performance gaps, CRM discipline, action plans.
- **SDR / BDR:** account research, pain hypotheses, outbound activity, qualification, concise messaging, learning and coaching.
- **BD / Partnerships:** portfolio ownership, problem diagnosis, offer design, negotiation, contract structure, partner growth.
- **Enterprise Sales:** account plans, multistakeholder alignment, partner leverage, forecast, complex economics, closing.

## 3. Writing standard

Prefer concrete sentences such as:

> 예산 제약으로 도입이 멈춘 고객에게 계약기간·월정액·이용요금 조합을 다시 설계해 내부 승인 가능한 안으로 전환했습니다.

Avoid:

- unsupported superlatives;
- repeating the largest metric in every section;
- passive noun-ending fragments that hide ownership;
- a solo-hero narrative;
- apologetic explanations for entering a lower title;
- vague claims about AI, data, transformation, or strategy.

## 4. CorePress portfolio

Use a six-to-eight-page structure unless a submission constraint requires less:

1. fictional educational POC label, scope, and team;
2. problem, users, and service context;
3. current and future workflow;
4. standard Salesforce capabilities versus limited customization;
5. Experience Cloud → Asset/Case → Field Service → limited Agentforce flow;
6. personally owned process, requirement, or Agentforce decisions;
7. team role, delivery, and presentation;
8. limitations, lessons, and credible next steps.

Guardrails:

- Place **FICTIONAL / EDUCATIONAL POC** where it cannot be missed.
- Do not invent customer, performance, adoption, certification, or revenue outcomes.
- Distinguish **designed**, **configured**, **implemented**, **presented**, and **proposed**.
- A polished brochure is not proof of delivery. Screenshots should support decisions and flows, not merely decorate pages.
- Show the user's contribution within the six-person team without erasing collaborators.

## 5. Company-specific design

When a branded portfolio or resume is useful:

1. inspect official design-system, brand, and developer documentation;
2. use exact published tokens where permitted;
3. use official logos and a small number of authentic components;
4. preserve ATS-readable hierarchy and text;
5. avoid visual guesses based only on brand mood;
6. render the final PDF and inspect every page at normal viewing size;
7. preserve an editable source separately.

Visual imitation must never outrank factual clarity or hiring relevance.

## 6. Cover letter logic

Answer four questions:

1. Why this function rather than only this company?
2. Which prior behaviors transfer directly?
3. What method, system, or domain will be learned here?
4. Why will the user remain and specialize after the transition?

For an SDR or BDR role, explicitly accept outbound work, meeting-generation or pipeline KPIs, coaching, and narrower scope. Do not rely on a promise of fast promotion.

## 7. Interview story bank

Prepare evidence for:

- discovering an unspoken customer problem;
- redesigning pricing or contract terms;
- recovering a stalled or at-risk relationship;
- coordinating delivery across functions;
- using data to change an operating decision;
- teaching, enabling, or aligning others;
- receiving coaching and changing behavior;
- choosing an intentional title or domain reset;
- explaining a failure, limitation, or weak result without evasion.

## 8. Deadline-aware planning

- Put confirmed fixed deadlines first.
- For rolling openings, set an aggressive internal deadline and label it as internal.
- During a critical project or presentation week, do not open several new resume lanes at once.
- Reuse evidence within a lane; do not reuse an unchanged narrative across lanes.
- Reserve a final block for fact consistency, English, ATS, links, and PDF rendering.
- If a relevant project is still changing, submit a resume-led application first only when the portfolio is optional; add the finished portfolio later where the process permits.
- When calendar execution is requested, create time blocks only after dates, time zone, and existing constraints are verified.

## 9. Submission gates

Do not submit until all applicable gates pass:

- at least four of the five most important job requirements are visible in the first page or primary application answers;
- every published number has scope, period, denominator, and ownership;
- the first three minutes of an interview can explain both **why move** and **why stay**;
- overqualification, direct-experience, and retention risks are answered;
- one primary application lane is unmistakable;
- CorePress is labeled as a fictional educational POC;
- company, title, location, requisition, and links are correct;
- resume, cover letter, portfolio, and application answers contain no factual contradictions;
- the PDF has been rendered and visually checked;
- official deadlines and internal deadlines are clearly distinguished.

If a gate fails, delay briefly to fix it unless the opening is likely to close. If it cannot be fixed honestly, do not submit.
