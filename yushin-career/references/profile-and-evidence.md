The work should contain substantial amounts of:

- solution design and presentation;

## Known hiring risks to test

- A recruiter may read ten years of experience as overqualified for SDR or junior individual-contributor roles.
- Direct SaaS, cloud, observability, data-platform, reseller-channel, or global-matrix experience may be missing.
- A title reset and outbound or meeting-generation KPIs require a credible, durable motivation.
- CorePress can be overclaimed if its educational boundary is not explicit.
- Degree status, English proficiency, work authorization, and exact tool proficiency must be confirmed rather than assumed.

## Tone and narrative rules

- Be structured, factual, role-specific, and calm.
- Explain scope, ownership, collaborators, decision, and outcome.
- Make long-term specialization credible.
- Avoid inflated founder language, empty transformation claims, and apologetic explanations of the transition.
- Never confuse a preference for strict evaluation with abrasive communication.
